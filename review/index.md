# Masarify for App Review

simulator. Each clip is the app itself — no narration, no editing, no titles.

If you only have time for one, watch **Reaching the subscription products**.

## Reaching the subscription products

No account, no sign-in, three taps from a cold launch.

1. Open the app and complete onboarding (every slide offers **Skip**).
2. On Home, tap the **gear** at the top right.
3. Tap **See plans** on the **Unlock Pro** card.

The paywall shows Monthly and Yearly with their App Store prices, the
auto-renew disclosure, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links, and Restore
Purchases.

<video controls playsinline width="320" src="./paywall.mp4"></video>

> There is no Masarify account, and no subscription state on our servers —
> entitlement is StoreKit-only. So there is no "account with an expired
> subscription" to hand over: **a fresh install is that state.**

## Notes for the reviewer

- **No sign-in is required** for anything except AI features and optional Google
  Drive backup. Sign in with Apple is offered wherever Google Sign-In is.
- **Account deletion** (Guideline 5.1.1(v)) is in the app: Settings → Account →
  Delete account. Users who never sign in can erase everything with
  Settings → Clear all data.
- **Microphone** is used only while a voice memo is recording. **Location** is
  optional, coarse only, and requested only on an explicit "Detect location".
- **Usage analytics are off by default** and require opt-in.
- This release is **iPhone only**.
